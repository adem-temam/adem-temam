## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📉 [Gradient Descent Visualizer](https://github.com/adem-temam/gradient-descent-visualizer)

> Interactive exploration of gradient-based optimization and loss landscapes.

Implemented and visualized optimization algorithms to understand how different update rules behave across challenging functions, including the effects of learning rates, curvature, saddle points, and ill-conditioned surfaces.

**Stack:** `Python` • `NumPy` • `Streamlit` • `Plotly` • `Pytest`

---

### 🎲 [Monte Carlo Decision Simulator](https://github.com/adem-temam/monte-carlo-decision)

> Exploring decision-making under uncertainty through stochastic simulation.

A simulation project focused on understanding distributions of possible outcomes rather than relying solely on deterministic averages. Includes uncertainty analysis, sensitivity experiments, and tail-risk measurements.

**Stack:** `Python` • `NumPy` • `Statistics` • `Monte Carlo`

---

### 📐 [Linear Algebra from Scratch](https://github.com/adem-temam/linear-algebra-from-scratch)

> Implementing fundamental linear algebra operations without relying on numerical libraries.

Built core operations including matrix multiplication, determinants, Gaussian elimination, matrix inversion, and rank computation to better understand the mathematical foundations used throughout machine learning.

**Stack:** `Python` • `Algorithms from Scratch` • `Linear Algebra`

---

### 🏎️ [F1 Race Strategy Simulator](https://github.com/adem-temam/f1_race_strategy_simulator)

> A data-driven simulation project exploring decision-making under uncertainty.

Uses real-world racing data as an application domain for studying simulation, optimization, and probabilistic decision-making.

**Stack:** `Python` • `Monte Carlo` • `Dynamic Programming` • `FastAPI`
