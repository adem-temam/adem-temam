### 🧠 About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate driven by **first-principles engineering**. Rather than treating machine learning and numerical optimization as plug-and-play black boxes, I focus on how the mechanics under the hood actually work:

- **Optimization Landscapes**: Analyzing how algorithms traverse ill-conditioned ravines, saddle points, and non-convex surfaces.
- **Stochastic Systems**: Quantifying uncertainty, tail risks, and decision boundaries using Monte Carlo simulations.
- **Real-World Calibration**: Ingesting live telemetry feeds and fitting empirical models to dynamic environments.

### 🚀 Featured Engineering Projects

#### 🏎️ [F1 Race Strategy Simulator](https://github.com/adem-temam/f1_race_strategy_simulator)
> **Data-driven simulation & optimization engine for Grand Prix strategy**
* **The Problem**: Discovering optimal pit windows and compound selections under non-linear tyre degradation and stochastic track events.
* **The Solution**: Formulated race progression as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) solved via Dynamic Programming backward induction in `< 15ms`, paired with OpenF1 real-world telemetry regression and an interactive dashboard.
* **Stack**: `Python` • `FastAPI` • `Dynamic Programming` • `Monte Carlo` • `Chart.js`

---

#### 📉 [Gradient Descent Visualizer](https://github.com/adem-temam/gradient-descent-visualizer)
> **Interactive 2D/3D testbench benchmarking first-order optimizers on non-convex loss surfaces**
* **The Problem**: Understanding why optimizers oscillate, stall at saddle points, or diverge on steep loss landscapes.
* **The Solution**: Implemented analytical gradients and update rules for SGD, Momentum, NAG, AdaGrad, RMSprop, and Adam with 2D contour maps and rotatable 3D surface trajectories. Includes an automated finite-difference test suite.
* **Stack**: `Python` • `NumPy` • `Streamlit` • `Plotly` • `Pytest`

---

#### 🎲 [Monte Carlo Decision Simulator](https://github.com/adem-temam/monte-carlo-decision)
> **Vectorized risk engine for decision-making under uncertainty**
* **The Problem**: Modeling high-variance outcomes where deterministic averages hide catastrophic downside risks.
* **The Solution**: High-throughput stochastic simulation engine providing tail risk metrics (Value-at-Risk, Expected Shortfall), Tornado sensitivity sweeps, and stochastic dominance comparisons.
* **Stack**: `Python` • `Vectorized Computation` • `Risk Modeling` • `Statistics`

---

#### 📐 [Linear Algebra from Scratch](https://github.com/adem-temam/linear-algebra-from-scratch)
> **Pure Python numerical engine built without external math libraries**
* **The Problem**: Gaining deep intuition into fundamental vector space transformations and linear solvers.
* **The Solution**: Built core linear algebra primitives from scratch: matrix products, determinants, Gaussian elimination, matrix inversion, and rank computation.
* **Stack**: `Python` • `Algorithms from Scratch` • `Matrix Operations`
